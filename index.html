Periodic Test 2

Info · How to use

Scope

Theoretical question banks for Periodic Test 2. Practical content is not included.

Quiz Interface

  • Medical Terminology — combining forms, the musculoskeletal, cardiovascular, blood and lymphatic, respiratory and nervous systems
  • Anatomy — bone identification: 32 bones and 2 girdles in 132 questions, plus 21 Relative Positions linking questions
  • English — Grammar Units 3-5, English for Medicine Unit 3 and writing skills
  • Medical Physics — Chapters 3-7 with formula practice · draft
  • Study Skills — Lectures 5-10 · draft

Picture Study

Medical Terminology pictures where every label is a blank. Drag words from the bank onto the boxes or onto the structures themselves, type the names, or show the labels. ↺ Reset starts a picture again.

Anatomy has its own Picture Study, opened from the Anatomy card: 3D views and axial, sagittal and coronal sections with a slider.

Tips

  • Keyboard on the main menu: ↑↓ move · Enter open
  • Choice questions: press 1–9 (number row or numpad with Num Lock on) or ↑ ↓ to highlight an option, then the same number again or Enter to choose
  • Multiple-answer questions: 1–9 tick or untick options · Enter checks
  • In a quiz run: ← → move between questions · Enter next · F fullscreen
  • Settings hold the theme, fonts, cursor, quiz order and the Timer box choices
  • Progress is saved in this browser on this device
